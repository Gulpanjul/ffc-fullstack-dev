## What Are the Different Link States, and Why Are They Important?

### Questions

</div>

1. What is the default state of a link?

   - [x] `:link`
   - [ ] `:visited`  
   - [ ] `:hover`  
   - [ ] `:active`  

2. Which state applies while a user is clicking the link?

   - [ ] `:link`  
   - [ ] `:visited`  
   - [ ] `:hover`  
   - [x] `:active`  

3. In what order should you style your links?

   - [ ] `visited`, `link`, `active`, `hover`.  
   - [ ] `link`, `active`, `hover`, `visited`.  
   - [ ] `hover`, `active`, `link`, `visited`.  
   - [x] `link`, `visited`, `hover`, `active`.
